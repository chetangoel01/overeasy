# Gemini 3.7 Live Social Imports

## Outcome

After correcting the caption-evidence gate, all five supplied sources produced
schema-valid recipes with `google/gemini-3.7-flash`. Every step ingredient
reference was valid. Four recipes were marked `ready`; the chili-garlic noodles
were marked `needsReview` because targeted verification disputed the stated
serving basis.

The first acquisition pass completed successfully but the old gate rejected all
five recipes before extraction. It ignored `metadata.description` even though
the extraction prompt accepts creator captions as lower-priority evidence. The
new gate accepts captions with at least three quantified ingredient mentions
when the combined caption/transcript contains cooking actions. Existing sparse
promotional captions remain rejected. Prompt/evidence version v12 prevents old
cache entries from being confused with corrected results.

## Extracted recipes

### 1. One Pot Creamy French Onion Pasta

- Source: `zachs.foods` TikTok `7612708181004799263`
- Review: `ready`; serving count estimated as 4 and explicitly uncertain
- Known cost: $0.015761; 3.7 processing: 7.340s

Ingredients: 3 large yellow onions; 2 tbsp butter; 1 tbsp vegetable oil; a
little water for deglazing; 8 oz pancetta; 2 garlic cloves; 2 tbsp white wine;
1 tsp salt; 1/2 tsp thyme; 1/2 tsp fresh rosemary; 8–10 oz dry pasta; 3 cups
beef broth; 1 cup heavy cream; 1 cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano; 1/2 cup
shredded Gruyère.

Steps:

1. Slice the onions and add them to a pot with the vegetable oil and butter.
2. Caramelize for 10–15 minutes, browning the onions and periodically
   deglazing with a small splash of water.
3. Add pancetta and cook until crisp. Stir in garlic and deglaze with white
   wine.
4. Add salt, thyme, rosemary, dry pasta, and beef broth; cook until the pasta
   is tender and the liquid has reduced.
5. Stir in heavy cream and Parmigiano-Reggiano, top with Gruyère, and serve.

### 2. “Wife me up!” Chili Garlic Noodles

- Source: `iankyo` TikTok `7436430114910506271`
- Review: `needsReview`; verifier found no explicit yield text supporting the
  model's stated serving basis
- Known cost: $0.018582; 3.7 processing: 12.345s

Ingredients: 2 portions noodles; 1/4 cup neutral oil; 3 tbsp chili flakes or
gochugaru; 2 tbsp soy sauce; 1 tbsp black vinegar; 1 tbsp sugar; 4 cloves
garlic; 1/2-inch knob ginger; 4 stalks green onion; sesame seeds to taste;
1/2 lb ground pork; salt to taste.

Steps:

1. Roughly chop garlic, grate ginger, and dice green onions.
2. Mix soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar, chili flakes or gochugaru, and sesame
   seeds.
3. Heat neutral oil and sauté garlic, ginger, and green onions until fragrant.
4. Add ground pork and salt; cook until browned.
5. Add the sauce and simmer with the pork for about 1 minute.
6. Add noodles and toss until fully coated.

### 3. Creamy Chicken & Gnocchi

- Source: `alexcookjoy` TikTok `7574621199519567136`
- Review: `ready`; stated yield 2; prep/cook/total 13/17/30 minutes
- Known cost: $0.016488; 3.7 processing: 10.544s

Ingredients: 1 lb chicken breast; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tbsp butter; 1 small
yellow onion; 3 cloves garlic; 1 cup heavy cream; 1 cup chicken broth; 1 tsp
Italian seasoning; 1/2 cup grated Parmesan; 1 lb potato gnocchi; 2 cups fresh
spinach; salt and pepper; unquantified white wine or water for deglazing;
optional crushed red pepper, parsley, and lemon zest.

Steps:

1. Heat oil and butter over medium-high heat. Season chicken and cook until
   golden, 5–6 minutes; remove.
2. Cook onion for 3 minutes, then garlic for 30 seconds. Deglaze, add broth,
   cream, and Italian seasoning, and bring to a gentle simmer.
3. Add gnocchi directly to the sauce and cook 4–5 minutes, stirring, until the
   sauce thickens.
4. Stir in Parmesan and spinach. Return chicken and simmer 1–2 minutes; adjust
   seasoning and add optional garnishes.

### 4. Hoisin Garlic Noodles

- Source: `foodiligence` TikTok `7581152180174966030`
- Review: `ready`; vegetable-oil amount uncertain; serving count estimated as 1
- Known cost: $0.016392; 3.7 processing: 8.427s

Ingredients: 14 oz dried wheat or egg noodles; 1/4 cup hoisin; 2 tbsp soy
sauce; 1 tbsp sesame oil; 2 tsp maple syrup or honey; unquantified vegetable
oil; 6 garlic cloves; 6 scallions; 4 tbsp toasted sesame seeds; salt and pepper.

Steps:

1. Boil noodles until al dente, then drain and rinse to cool.
2. Mix hoisin, soy sauce, sesame oil, and maple syrup or honey.
3. Heat vegetable oil; cook garlic and white scallion parts until fragrant.
4. Add sauce and noodles and toss to coat.
5. Leave undisturbed for 2–3 minutes until crisp underneath; season.
6. Top with sesame seeds and green scallion parts.

## Quality assessment

The instructions are coherent, ordered, source-grounded, and preserve stated
timers. The model correctly repaired the transcript's “news” mishearing to
“noodles” using the caption, retained the unquantified deglazing liquid as an
uncertainty, and did not invent missing cook times.

Two downstream quality issues remain visible:

- The deterministic review layer estimates servings and can still mark the
  recipe `ready`. Its estimates of 1 serving for 14 oz noodles and four
  cucumbers are not credible enough for quality-first behavior.
- Nutrition is null on all five recipes. USDA calculation intentionally refuses
  a recipe when any material ingredient lacks a safely convertible quantity or
  unique food match. The explicit chicken-gnocchi nutrition panel was also not
  recovered because its “per serving” marker appears after, rather than before,
  the macro values.

These are post-extraction policy/parser issues, not failures of Gemini 3.7's
ingredient or step extraction.
